30-day growth strategy

  1. Week 1: Audit landing pages, configure conversion tracking and establish baseline metrics.
  2. Week 2: Test search ad copy and two social creatives with consistent offers.
  3. Week 3: Compare qualified leads and cost per acquisition; shift budget cautiously toward stronger channels.
  4. Week 4: Review booked jobs, revenue attribution and customer feedback; report findings and next tests.

Example ad copy

Need help with home maintenance in Tucson? Get a clear estimate and a convenient appointment. Request a quote today.

Measurement plan

Track campaign tags, quote submissions, qualified leads, booked jobs and attributed revenue. Verify tracking before interpreting performance.
